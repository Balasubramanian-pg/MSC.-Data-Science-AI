As I was saying, the whole aim of a dashboard is to drive action from the insights that 
you provide. 
  
And always remember the type of variables, the data types that you have in the data. 
Always remember the different types of data that you have on the data set and how 
they are going to interact through different visuals on the dashboard. So, for this, I 
have taken the example of a dashboard from our Ministry of External Affairs, 
Government of India. 
  
So, this dashboard is very interesting and very interactive. Here, for example, I have 
selected citizen services and you can play around by selecting trade and commerce 
or diaspora engagement. It will show the relevant parameters and the source is given 
here. 
  
So, what are the categorical data that you see here? First of all, citizen services, trade 
and commerce, diaspora engagement is one level of categorical variable on which you 
have different types of services that are rendered by the Ministry of External Affairs. 
While I click this citizen services, it shows the visas granted, passports issued, mortal 
remains brought back, pilgrims assisted, so on and so forth. It has about seven 
categories in which these services are provided. 
  
And again, you have the quantitative elements like the number of visas issued, 
passports issued, clearly depicted with the use of infographics, small elements, clearly 
giving you a picture of what is this services related to and what is the number of the 
services rendered by it. And this is the dashboard that it is there in the website when 
you take the first look. This is one level of information that is provided to you. 
  
Now, once you want to go deep onto this, I click on visas granted, I end up on a 
dashboard which is like this. In this, you have a time series showing the visa issuances 
over a period of time, both regular visa, e-visa and number of e-visa countries are 
increasing over a period of time. And the pattern again in COVID, you can see there 
is a clear dip and that has been rising. 
  
And what is most interesting here is the comparison is through time series graph, but 
also through the clear text box that shows that there is 4% decrease in visas issued in 
2023-2024 compared to the previous period. And it also has a status thing, same as 
a text box saying that three days is the average issuance time for e-visa. Now, if you 
remember, simple text is also a very powerful visualisation tool that we have discussed 
in our earlier classes. 
  
Here, they have simply used a simple text with a small numerical element saying that 
it is 4% decreased with the comparative time period. All of this is coming as a text box 
on the visual, automated of course. So, you can clearly bring your attention of the 
audience to two or three topics.
