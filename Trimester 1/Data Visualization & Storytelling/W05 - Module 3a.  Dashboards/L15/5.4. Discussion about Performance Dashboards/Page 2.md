First is it is a graphical and abstracted data. So you have set of visuals which abstract the 
underlying processes so that key insights can be extracted by looking at those visuals. So you 
have typical graphs, symbols and charts. 
  
You can have a graph showing the growth in revenue over a period of time or you can have a 
gauge diagram showing the percentage of target that has been reached. You have a map 
showing the differential in the colour saturation showing the target achievement or the 
growth rates like we have seen in the GDP growth case. This enables monitoring. 
  
You can clearly see where is the gap or who are the laggards or who are the super performers. 
Then you have summarised dimensional data. Once you look at the regions that are 
performing very well or not performing, you can drill down on them to look at which particular 
departments or products or sections they are lagging in or they are outperforming. 
  
What is the reason for the performance or non-performance? Then once you identify them, 
you can also have detailed operational level queries integrated into the performance 
dashboard depending upon where are you using it and try to understand what is the cause, 
how to improve the performance so on and so forth. And this can be used for reporting. 
Reports can then be analysed and then can be monitored. 
  
With all this, you can have a very good planning layer which can ensure your strategy 
execution is done on time with mid-core corruption in an objective and transparent fashion. 
Therefore, performance dashboards, designing of performance dashboards becomes a critical 
skill for the business analyst teams so that they can talk to the top management about the 
strategic objectives, they can talk to the operational guys, talk and see where are the things 
needs to be improved, they can show where things can be improved. However, for designing 
operational dashboards, you need a very clear understanding of three things. 
  
One, the strategic objectives, second, the data pipeline from which the different types of data 
have come and the effective visualisation tools so that you can bring all these insights into 
one place which is understood by everyone in the process. We will look at a dashboard 
building for a performance setup in our experiential learning wherein we will try to bring these 
elements and create a performance dashboard highlighting the action to insight paradigm. I 
would give an example of performance dashboard. 
  
Again, I am taking the Vahan dashboard which is the dashboard of Ministry of Road, Transport 
and Highways, Government of India. It shows the pending application dashboard. There are 
several dashboards in the Vahan portal and just focussing on the pending application 
dashboard for Delhi I have selected and the dashboard clearly shows the transaction-wise 
pending transactions, top 15 RPOs and the registration type-wise, the pending registration 
for top 15 RPOs and you have the numbers given here, clearly following the gestalt principles, 
ordered in a logical order so you can clearly see which is the topmost pending category.
