## Question 1

**Correct Answer: Parse**

Eliminate the wrong answers:

* **Filter** ❌ Removes irrelevant data but does not structure it.
* **Mine** ❌ Discovers patterns or extracts information from datasets.
* **Acquire** ❌ Refers to obtaining or collecting the data.

**Remaining correct option:**

* **Parse** ✅

## Question 2

**Correct Answer: Distribution**

Eliminate the wrong answers:

* **Relationship** ❌ Used to study associations between variables.
* **Composition** ❌ Shows how parts contribute to a whole.
* **Comparison** ❌ Used to compare values across categories.

**Remaining correct option:**

* **Distribution** ✅
