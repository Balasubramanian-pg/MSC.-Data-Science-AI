# 4.1 Text-Based Representation

The lecture presents election turnout information in paragraph form.

### Key Numbers Mentioned

- Eligible voters increased by **7.4%** compared to 2019
    
- Voter turnout declined from:
    
    - **67% → 66%**
        
- National average turnout:
    
    - **66%**
        
- Lakshadweep turnout:
    
    - **84%**
        
- Bihar turnout:
    
    - **56%**
        
- Assam:
    
    - Above 80%
        
- Uttar Pradesh:
    
    - Less than 60%
        

### Problem with Textual Representation

The audience must:

- remember multiple numbers
    
- compare percentages mentally
    
- identify outliers manually
    

This increases cognitive load.
