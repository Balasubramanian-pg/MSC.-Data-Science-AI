# 6.1 Visualization and Narrative Building

The lecture argues that visualization is fundamentally about storytelling.

A visualization should answer:

- What happened?
    
- Why did it happen?
    
- What should be done next?
    

### Effective Storytelling Includes

- context
    
- comparison
    
- trends
    
- emphasis
    
- clarity
    

Visualization converts raw numbers into narrative structure.
