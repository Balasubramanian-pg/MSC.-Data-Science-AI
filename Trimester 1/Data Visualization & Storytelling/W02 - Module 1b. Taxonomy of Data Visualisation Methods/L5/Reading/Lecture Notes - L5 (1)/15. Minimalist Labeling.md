# 15. Minimalist Labeling

### Purpose

Reduce clutter while maintaining clarity.

### Poor Example

Labeling every single point.

```text
10
12
14
15
16
17
18
19
```

This quickly becomes overwhelming.

### Better Example

Label only:

* Start point
* End point
* Peak value
* Important milestone

### Benefits

* Cleaner visuals
* Faster interpretation
* Stronger emphasis
