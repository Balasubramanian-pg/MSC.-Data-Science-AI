# Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1

Assuming data automatically drives decisions.

### Mistake 2

Creating charts without understanding the audience.

### Mistake 3

Selecting visuals before defining the message.

### Mistake 4

Using scatter plots when a simple comparison is needed.

### Mistake 5

Overloading charts with annotations and colors.

### Mistake 6

Ignoring how the visualization will be consumed.

### Mistake 7

Presenting findings without recommendations.
