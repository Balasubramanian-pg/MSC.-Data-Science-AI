# Examination Notes

### What is the ultimate goal of data visualization?

To drive understanding and business action.

### What is audience-centric storytelling?

Designing visualizations around audience needs rather than data availability.

### What are the stages of the insight-to-action pipeline?

1. Data
2. Exploration
3. Insights
4. Storytelling
5. Action

### What screenplay structure is recommended for visual storytelling?

1. Incident
2. Rising Action
3. Climax
4. Resolution

### Which chart is best for trends?

Line Chart.

### Which chart is best for relationships?

Scatter Plot.

### Which chart is best for comparing quantities?

Bar Chart.

### What are pre-attentive attributes?

Visual properties that attract attention immediately before conscious processing.
