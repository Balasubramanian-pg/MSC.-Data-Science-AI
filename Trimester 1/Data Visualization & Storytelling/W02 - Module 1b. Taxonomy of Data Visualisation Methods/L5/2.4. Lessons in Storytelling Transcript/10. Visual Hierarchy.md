# 10. Visual Hierarchy

### Definition

Visual hierarchy is the arrangement of elements according to importance.

The audience should know:

* Where to look first
* What is most important
* What is secondary

### Example

Poor hierarchy:

```text
Everything appears equally important
```

Good hierarchy:

```text
KEY INSIGHT

Supporting Evidence

Additional Context
```

### Benefits

* Faster understanding
* Reduced cognitive effort
* Better retention
