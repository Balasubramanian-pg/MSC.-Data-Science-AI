# Practical Storytelling Checklist

Before presenting a visualization, ask:

### Context

* Who is the audience?
* What do they care about?

### Purpose

* What action should result?

### Medium

* Will I be present to explain it?

### Visual Choice

* Does the chart support the message?

### Narrative

* Is there a clear beginning, middle, and conclusion?

### Design

* Have I highlighted only what matters?

### Simplicity

* Can anything be removed?
