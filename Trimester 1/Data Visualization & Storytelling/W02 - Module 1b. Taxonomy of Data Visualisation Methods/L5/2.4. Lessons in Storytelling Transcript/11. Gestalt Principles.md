# 11. Gestalt Principles

Gestalt psychology explains how humans naturally organize visual information.

### Core Idea

People perceive patterns before individual elements.

### Examples

#### Proximity

Objects close together appear related.

#### Similarity

Objects sharing color or shape appear grouped.

#### Continuity

The eye naturally follows connected paths.

#### Enclosure

Objects within boundaries appear associated.

[!IMPORTANT]

Good visualizations work with natural human perception rather than against it.
