# 4. The Screenplay Model of Data Storytelling

The lecture compares effective visualization to a screenplay.

Just as movies follow a structured narrative, visual stories should follow a predictable flow.

The recommended structure is:

```text
Incident
    ↓
Rising Action
    ↓
Climax
    ↓
Resolution
```

### Incident (The Challenge)

What problem exists?

Examples:

* Customer churn increased.
* Revenue declined.
* Costs exceeded budget.

### Rising Action (The Analysis)

Present supporting evidence.

Examples:

* Trend analysis
* Regional comparisons
* Customer segmentation

### Climax (The Core Insight)

Reveal the most important finding.

Examples:

* 70% of churn originates from one customer segment.
* Revenue decline is concentrated in one product category.

### Resolution (The Recommendation)

Explain what should happen next.

Examples:

* Launch retention programs.
* Increase staffing.
* Reallocate budget.

[!TIP]

A visualization without a recommendation is often incomplete from a business perspective.
