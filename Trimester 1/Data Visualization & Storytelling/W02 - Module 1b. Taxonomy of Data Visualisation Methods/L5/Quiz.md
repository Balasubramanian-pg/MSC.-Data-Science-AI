## Question 1

**Correct Answer: Ensuring every element, like color and alignment, serves a clear purpose.**

Eliminate the wrong answers:

* **Using as many colors as possible to make the visual appealing.** ❌ Excessive colors can distract and reduce clarity.
* **Making the chart complex to show the depth of the data.** ❌ Effective visualizations prioritize clarity, not complexity.
* **Tailoring visuals for a single, generic audience type.** ❌ Visuals should be tailored to the specific audience and context.

**Remaining correct option:**

* **Ensuring every element, like color and alignment, serves a clear purpose.** ✅

## Question 2

**Correct Answer: To move the audience from data to insight, and then to action.**

Eliminate the wrong answers:

* **To show as much raw data as possible.** ❌ Data storytelling focuses on key insights, not overwhelming the audience with data.
* **To create the most aesthetically pleasing chart.** ❌ Aesthetics support the story but are not the ultimate goal.
* **To let the audience interpret the data without guidance.** ❌ Data storytelling provides guidance and narrative to drive understanding.

**Remaining correct option:**

* **To move the audience from data to insight, and then to action.** ✅
