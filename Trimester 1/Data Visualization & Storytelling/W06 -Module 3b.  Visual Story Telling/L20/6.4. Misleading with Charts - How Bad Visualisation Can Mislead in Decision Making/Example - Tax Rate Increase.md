# Example: Tax Rate Increase

The example compares:

- tax rate increasing from 35% to 39.6%
    

Numerically:

- increase = 4.6 percentage points
    

But visually:

- the first chart makes the increase appear enormous
    

Why?

Because the axis only spans:

- 34% to 40%
    

Instead of:

- 0% to 40%
