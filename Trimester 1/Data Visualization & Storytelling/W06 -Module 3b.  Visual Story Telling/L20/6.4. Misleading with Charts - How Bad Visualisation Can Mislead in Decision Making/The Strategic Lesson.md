# The Strategic Lesson

> Data storytelling is inseparable from framing.

The way analysts:

- order information
    
- group data
    
- sequence drill-downs
    
- choose subsets
    
- define hierarchies
    

fundamentally changes:

- audience perception
    
- narrative interpretation
    
- decision outcomes
    

This means:  
analytics is not only mathematical.

It is:

- psychological
    
- structural
    
- rhetorical
