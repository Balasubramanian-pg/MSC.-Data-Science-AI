# Flowcharts

Flowcharts communicate:

- processes
    
- sequences
    
- dependencies
    

Example:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Raw Data] --> B[Visualization]
    B --> C[Narrative Framing]
    C --> D[Audience Interpretation]
    D --> E[Decision Making]
```

Flowcharts are especially useful when explaining:

- business workflows
    
- causal relationships
    
- operational systems
