# Why Most Dashboards Fail

Most dashboards focus only on:

- metrics
    
- visuals
    
- interactivity
    

But ignore:

- narrative structure
    
- audience psychology
    
- contextual framing
    

This creates:

> informational density without persuasive clarity.

Users see data.  
But they do not know:

- what matters
    
- why it matters
    
- what changed
    
- what action should follow
