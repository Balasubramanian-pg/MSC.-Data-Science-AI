# Why Narrative Wrapping Matters

The lecture states:

> Visuals must be wrapped in a narrative.

This is exactly right.

Without narrative:

- visuals remain fragmented,
    
- interpretation becomes inconsistent,
    
- audience attention weakens.
    

Narratives provide:

- sequence,
    
- focus,
    
- causality,
    
- emotional relevance,
    
- and decision framing.
