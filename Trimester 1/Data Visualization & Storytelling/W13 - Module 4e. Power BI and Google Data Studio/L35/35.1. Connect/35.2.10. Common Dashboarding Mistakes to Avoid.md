# 35.2.10. Common Dashboarding Mistakes to Avoid

Even with advanced tools like Power BI and Google Looker Studio, visual designers frequently introduce errors that mislead decision-makers.

### 10.1 Key Warnings

>[!Warning]
> **Unweighted Averaging:** Never calculate the average of transaction ratios (such as profit margin percentages) directly. Always calculate the weighted aggregate using total sums:
> $$ \text{Profit Percentage} = \frac{\sum \text{Profit}}{\sum \text{Sales}} $$
> Failing to do so can severely distort performance metrics for regions with high-volume, low-margin sales.

>[!Warning]
> **Visual Clutter and Lack of Borders:** Do not crowd too many widgets into a single workspace. Without adequate spacing and border shadows, separate visualizations merge into a confusing mass of color, making it difficult for the eye to distinguish between different data stories.

>[!Warning]
> **Misaligned Color Context:** Avoid using highly saturated dark colors across all charts simultaneously, as it fatigues the user. Additionally, never use red for positive metrics or green for negative metrics, as this directly violates universal visual conventions and causes immediate user confusion.
