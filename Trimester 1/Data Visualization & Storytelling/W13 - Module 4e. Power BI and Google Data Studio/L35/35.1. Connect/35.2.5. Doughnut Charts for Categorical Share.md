# 35.2.5. Doughnut Charts for Categorical Share

To display how different categories or regions contribute to the total sales volume, we employ part-to-whole visualizations like doughnut charts. A doughnut chart functions similarly to a standard pie chart but features a hollow center, which improves readability and leaves room for displaying central callouts such as total metrics.

The following table shows the distribution of sales and metrics across different geographic regions as displayed in a regional sales doughnut chart.

| Region | Sales Share | Performance Level |
|----------|:----------:|----------:|
| West | 35% | High |
| East | 30% | High |
| Central | 20% | Moderate |
| South | 15% | Low |

By using a doughnut chart, the user can easily observe that the West and East regions together make up more than half of the total sales. In the dashboard, we can configure these charts to show the percentage of sales rather than the raw record count, ensuring that the metric aligns with the core business goals.
