# 35.2.6. Geographical Mapping for Spatial Analysis

While regional share provides a high-level view, businesses need to analyze local variations to make distribution and pricing decisions. A geographical choropleth map provides spatial context by highlighting individual administrative boundaries, such as states, based on a specific performance metric.

In our dashboard, the map displays the calculated state-by-state profit percentage:

$$
\text{Profit Percentage} = \frac{\sum \text{Profit}}{\sum \text{Sales}}
$$

By using a green-to-red or light-to-dark color gradient, the map immediately signals which states are generating high margins and which states are operating at a loss. If a user selects a specific state on this map, the surrounding charts instantly filter to that state's data, allowing analysts to investigate localized operational issues.
