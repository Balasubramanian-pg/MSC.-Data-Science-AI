# 35.2.1. From Aggregate Metrics to Decision Insights

Raw transactional data represents the ultimate source of truth, but its sheer volume makes it incomprehensible to decision-makers. Visual dashboards solve this problem by converting hundreds of thousands of individual rows into structured visual components. 

In any typical sales database, a point estimate like the total sales must be represented dynamically to show high-level trends alongside granular variations. These visual summaries allow analysts to immediately detect geographic variations, product-specific deficits, and categorical performance trends.
