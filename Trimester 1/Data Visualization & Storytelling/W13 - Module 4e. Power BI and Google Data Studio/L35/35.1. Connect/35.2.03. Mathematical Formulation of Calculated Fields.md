# 35.2.3. Mathematical Formulation of Calculated Fields

When designing interactive dashboards, we often need to present complex ratios that are not stored in the underlying data source. For instance, calculating a state's overall profitability requires a calculated field to evaluate the cumulative performance of all transactions within that state.

A common error is taking the average of individual transaction margins, which fails to account for varying transaction volumes. Instead, we must define the weighted aggregate profit percentage.

The formula for the calculated field **Profit Percentage** is:

$$
\text{Profit Percentage} = \frac{\sum \text{Profit}}{\sum \text{Sales}}
$$

where:

- $$ \text{Profit Percentage} $$ = the overall weighted profitability ratio

- $$ \sum \text{Profit} $$ = the sum of profits across all filtered transactions

- $$ \sum \text{Sales} $$ = the sum of sales across all filtered transactions

This formula represents a ratio of sums rather than a sum of ratios. By using:

$$
\text{Profit Percentage} = \frac{\sum \text{Profit}}{\sum \text{Sales}}
$$

the dashboard ensures that large-scale, high-value transactions correctly weight the final percentage, maintaining mathematical accuracy regardless of how the user filters or drills down into the data.
