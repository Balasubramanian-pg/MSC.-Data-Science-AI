# 35.2.6. Doughnut Charts for Categorical Share Estimation

To display how different categories or regions contribute to the total sales volume, we employ part-to-whole visualizations like doughnut charts. A doughnut chart is geometrically identical to a pie chart but features a blank center, which improves readability and provides a clean visual boundary.

The share of any given subcategory is calculated as:

$$
\text{Share} = \frac{\text{Sales}_{\text{Subcategory}}}{\sum \text{Sales}}
$$

Suppose:
- Subcategory A Sales is:

$$
400
$$

- Subcategory B Sales is:

$$
600
$$

- Subcategory C Sales is:

$$
1000
$$

### Step 1: Sum Overall Sales
Calculate the denominator by summing all subcategory sales:

$$
\sum \text{Sales} = 400 + 600 + 1000 = 2000
$$

### Step 2: Identify Target Category Sales
Identify the sales of the target subcategory (Subcategory C):

$$
\text{Sales}_{\text{Subcategory C}} = 1000
$$

### Step 3: Apply the Share Formula
Set up the proportion equation:

$$
\text{Share} = \frac{\text{Sales}_{\text{Subcategory C}}}{\sum \text{Sales}}
$$

### Step 4: Compute Decimal Ratio
Substitute the values and evaluate the division:

$$
\text{Share} = \frac{1000}{2000} = 0.50
$$

### Step 5: Convert and Express as Percentage
Multiply by 100 to find the final share value:

$$
0.50 \times 100 = 50\%
$$

The final share value of Subcategory C is **50%**.
