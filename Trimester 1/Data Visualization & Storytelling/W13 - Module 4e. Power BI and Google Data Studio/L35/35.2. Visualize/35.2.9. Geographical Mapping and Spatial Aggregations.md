# 35.2.9. Geographical Mapping and Spatial Aggregations

Geographical maps display spatial variations by coloring administrative boundaries (such as states or countries) according to a chosen metric. In our dashboard, the geographical map displays the calculated state-by-state profit percentage:

$$
\text{Profit Percentage} = \frac{\sum \text{Profit}}{\sum \text{Sales}}
$$

Suppose:
- State A has total profit of:

$$
150
$$

- State A has total sales of:

$$
1200
$$

- State B has total profit of:

$$
350
$$

- State B has total sales of:

$$
1800
$$

### Step 1: Sum Total Regional Profit
Combine the profits of both states to find the numerator:

$$
\sum \text{Profit} = 150 + 350 = 500
$$

### Step 2: Sum Total Regional Sales
Combine the sales of both states to find the denominator:

$$
\sum \text{Sales} = 1200 + 1800 = 3000
$$

### Step 3: State Aggregate Profit Percentage Formula
Define the weighted calculation formula:

$$
\text{Profit Percentage} = \frac{\sum \text{Profit}}{\sum \text{Sales}}
$$

### Step 4: Divide Aggregate Profit by Aggregate Sales
Substitute the computed sums to find the decimal ratio:

$$
\text{Profit Percentage} = \frac{500}{3000} \approx 0.1667
$$

### Step 5: Convert and Express as Percentage
Multiply the ratio by 100 to get the final regional profit percentage:

$$
0.1667 \times 100 \approx 16.67\%
$$

The overall weighted profit percentage for the combined region is **16.67%**.
