# 35.2.7. Why Simple Percentages Are Rare in Complex Systems

In advanced business intelligence, evaluating simple metrics like record counts or unweighted averages is rarely sufficient. When analyzing financial performance, we must build calculated fields to evaluate weighted ratios. 

If we simply averaged the profit margins of all transactions, a small transaction with a high profit margin would distort the aggregate performance. This necessitating the use of weighted calculated fields.

We estimate the true profitability of a state or category using the weighted aggregate profit percentage formula:

$$
\text{Profit Percentage} = \frac{\sum \text{Profit}}{\sum \text{Sales}}
$$
