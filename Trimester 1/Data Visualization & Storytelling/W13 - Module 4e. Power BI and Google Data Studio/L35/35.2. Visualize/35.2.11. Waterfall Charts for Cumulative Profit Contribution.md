# 35.2.11. Waterfall Charts for Cumulative Profit Contribution

While tree maps and doughnut charts excel at showing volume, a waterfall chart is the optimal visualization for displaying how individual categories add to or subtract from a cumulative total. This is valuable for tracking profit contributions.

In a waterfall chart:
- The initial bar represents starting values or the largest positive contributor.
- Floating bars represent the sequential positive (green) or negative (red) contributions of each category.
- The final bar represents the total aggregated profit:

$$
\text{Total Profit} = \sum \text{Profit}
$$

By displaying these components in descending order of contribution, executives can immediately identify which subcategories are generating profits and which are causing losses.
