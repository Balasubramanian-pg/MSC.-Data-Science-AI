# 35.2.13. Frequentist vs Bayesian Interpretation of Dynamic Data

Classic business dashboards can be interpreted from two different statistical perspectives:

- **Frequentist Viewpoint:** The underlying database parameters (such as the true population profit margin) are fixed. The active dashboard filters act as random samples. The goal is to determine if the observed variations are statistically significant or simply random noise.
- **Bayesian Viewpoint:** The current view of the dashboard represents our prior knowledge. As we apply filters or stream live data refreshes through native SQL server connections, we update our beliefs and refine our understanding of operational performance.

Integrating real-time, automated data refreshes allows organizations to continuously update their analytical models as new transaction telemetry is collected.
