# Why Multi-Plot Layouts Matter

Suppose you plot:

- temperature
    
- rainfall
    

on the same graph.

The result may become visually confusing because:

- scales differ
    
- units differ
    
- patterns overlap
    
- interpretation becomes difficult
    

Subplots solve this problem by:

- separating variables visually
    
- preserving alignment
    
- enabling comparison
    
- reducing clutter
