# 3.0.1. The Concept of Data Quality

Data quality is the foundation of any analytical or modeling endeavor. The principle of "garbage in, garbage out" dictates that the quality of your outputs is strictly bounded by the quality of your inputs. 

In statistical modelling, data quality refers to the degree to which a dataset accurately represents the true state of the phenomenon being measured. High-quality data is accurate, complete, consistent, and reliable. Low-quality data introduces errors that propagate through every subsequent step of the analysis, leading to misleading conclusions and poor model performance.

To emphasize the foundational rule of data quality, we state it formally:

$$
\text{Output Quality} \leq \text{Input Quality}
$$
