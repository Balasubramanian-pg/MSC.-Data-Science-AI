# 6.4.6. Why Feature Selection Matters

Eliminating irrelevance and redundancy is not just an academic exercise; it has profound practical implications.

| Benefit | Explanation | System Impact |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Better Performance | Removes statistical noise | Higher Model Accuracy |
| Lower Complexity | Requires fewer computations | Faster Inference Speed |
| Better Interpretability | Results in simpler models | Improved Business Usability |
| Reduced Overfitting | Avoids learning irrelevant patterns | Greater Generalization |
| Faster Training | Reduces the overall feature space | Lower Cloud Compute Costs |

Feature selection acts as a dual-purpose optimization, improving both computational efficiency and overall prediction quality.
