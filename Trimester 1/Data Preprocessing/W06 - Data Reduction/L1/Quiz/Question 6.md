# Question 6

**Question:** In the context of predicting a student's GPA, which of the following is the best example of an irrelevant feature?

* **Eliminated Options:**

  * *The number of hours the student studies per week:* Study hours are directly related to academic performance and are likely predictive.
  * *The student's attendance record:* Attendance often correlates with academic success.
  * *The student's grades in previous semesters:* Previous grades are typically strong predictors of future GPA.

* **Correct Answer:** **The student's unique ID number**

> [!NOTE]
> **Explanation:**
>
> A student's ID number is simply an identifier:
>
> $$
> \text{Student ID} \not\rightarrow \text{Academic Performance}
> $$
>
> It carries no meaningful information about the student's GPA and is therefore considered an **irrelevant feature**.
