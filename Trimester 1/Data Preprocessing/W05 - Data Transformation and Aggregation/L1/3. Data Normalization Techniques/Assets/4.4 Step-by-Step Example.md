# 4.4 Step-by-Step Example

Suppose:

|Marks|
|---|
|35|
|65|
|90|

Mean becomes:

$$
\mu \approx 63.33
$$

Assume:

$$
\sigma = 10
$$

Now normalize 35:

z = \frac{35-63.33}{10}

Similarly:

|Original Value|Z-Score|
|---|---|
|65|0.5|
|90|3|

The transformed values now represent relative statistical positioning rather than raw magnitude.
