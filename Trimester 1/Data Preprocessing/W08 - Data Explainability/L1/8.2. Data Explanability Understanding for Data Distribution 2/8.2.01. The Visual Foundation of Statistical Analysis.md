# 8.2.1. The Visual Foundation of Statistical Analysis

Transitioning from numerical summaries to graphical tools, we must recognize that visual structure reveals statistical behavior. 

- While measures like the mean and standard deviation provide concise summaries, they can obscure the underlying geometry of the data.
- Graphical tools help us understand how data behaves, exposing patterns that numbers alone cannot capture.

The central theme of exploratory data analysis is that statistical understanding begins with visual understanding. 

Before applying machine learning algorithms or statistical tests, we must visualize the data to understand its geometry, study its spread, detect skewness, and identify outliers.
