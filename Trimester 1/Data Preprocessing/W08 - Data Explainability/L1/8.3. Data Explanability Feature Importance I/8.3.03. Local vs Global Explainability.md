# 8.3.3. Local vs Global Explainability

To systematically address the opacity of black box models, we must distinguish between two types of explainability.

### 3.1 Local Explainability

Local explainability focuses on explaining **one specific prediction**. 
- It answers questions such as why a specific loan was denied or why a specific image was classified as cancerous. 

### 3.2 Global Explainability

Global explainability focuses on the **overall model** or data behavior. 
- It explains the entire dataset, overall feature importance, and general model trends.
- Data distribution analysis, as discussed in previous sections, is a primary form of global explainability.
