# 8.3.2. The Tradeoff Between Accuracy and Interpretability

The lecture introduces a fundamental machine learning tradeoff. As model complexity increases, predictive power generally increases, but interpretability decreases. 

Simple models, such as linear regression and decision trees, are highly interpretable because their internal mechanics are transparent. <br>
Complex models, such as deep learning, support vector machines, and ensemble systems, are often entirely opaque. 

The goal of explainability is not about building the models themselves. <br>
It is about understanding why predictions happen, specifically identifying which features contributed most to a given prediction.
