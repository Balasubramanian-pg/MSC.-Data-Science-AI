# 9.2.6. Example of Computing Communality and Uniqueness

Suppose:
- A specific survey question represents an observed variable.
- The factor analysis model extracts two latent factors.
- The loading on the first factor is:
  $$\lambda_{j1} = 0.8$$
- The loading on the second factor is:
  $$\lambda_{j2} = 0.5$$

### Step 1: Identify the Communality Formula
$$h_j^2 = \lambda_{j1}^2 + \lambda_{j2}^2$$

### Step 2: Extract the Given Loadings
$$\lambda_{j1} = 0.8$$
and
$$\lambda_{j2} = 0.5$$

### Step 3: Square the Loadings
$$0.8^2 = 0.64$$
and
$$0.5^2 = 0.25$$

### Step 4: Compute the Sum of Squared Loadings
$$0.64 + 0.25 = 0.89$$

### Step 5: Calculate the Final Uniqueness
$$1 - 0.89 = 0.11$$
