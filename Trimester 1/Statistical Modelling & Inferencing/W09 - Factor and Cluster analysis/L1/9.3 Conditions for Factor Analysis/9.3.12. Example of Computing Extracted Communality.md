# 9.3.12. Example of Computing Extracted Communality

Suppose:
- An observed variable loads onto two extracted latent factors.
- The calculated loading on the first factor is:
  $$\lambda_{1} = 0.70$$
- The calculated loading on the second factor is:
  $$\lambda_{2} = -0.40$$

### Step 1: State the Communality Formula
$$h^2 = \lambda_1^2 + \lambda_2^2$$

### Step 2: Extract the Given Loadings
$$0.70$$ 
and 
$$-0.40$$

### Step 3: Square the Individual Loadings
$$(0.70)^2 = 0.49$$
and
$$(-0.40)^2 = 0.16$$

### Step 4: Sum the Squared Loadings
$$0.49 + 0.16$$

### Step 5: Calculate the Final Communality
**$$h^2 = 0.65$$**
(The retained factors successfully explain 65% of this variable's total variance).
