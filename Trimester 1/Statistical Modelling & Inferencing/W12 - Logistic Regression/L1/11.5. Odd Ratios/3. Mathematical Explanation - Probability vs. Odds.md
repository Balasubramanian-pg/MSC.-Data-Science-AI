# 3. Mathematical Explanation: Probability vs. Odds

Before defining the Odds Ratio, we must formally separate Probability from Odds.

Let $p$ be the probability of an event occurring ($P(Y=1)$).
*   **Probability Space:** Bounded between $[0, 1]$.
*   **Odds Space:** The ratio of the probability of success to the probability of failure. Bounded between $[0, \infty)$.

$$
\text{Odds}(Y=1) = \frac{p}{1 - p}
$$

> [!NOTE]
> If $p = 0.8$, the odds are $0.8 / 0.2 = 4$. We say the odds are "4 to 1".
> If $p = 0.2$, the odds are $0.2 / 0.8 = 0.25$. We say the odds are "1 to 4".
