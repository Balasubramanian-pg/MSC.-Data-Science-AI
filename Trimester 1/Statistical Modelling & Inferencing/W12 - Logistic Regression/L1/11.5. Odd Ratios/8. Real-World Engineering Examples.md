# 8. Real-World Engineering Examples

### Example 1: Credit Scoring (Finance)
In credit risk modeling, models often must comply with the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) and be fully interpretable. If a Logistic Regression model determines that the coefficient for `Missed_Payment_Count` is $-1.2$, the bank translates this to: $e^{-1.2} = 0.30$. 
*Interpretation provided to regulators:* "For every missed payment, a customer's odds of being approved for a loan are reduced by 70%."

### Example 2: E-commerce A/B Testing
When predicting if a user will "Add to Cart" ($1$) or not ($0$), an indicator variable for `Saw_Red_Button` might have a coefficient of $0.22$. 
*Interpretation provided to Product Managers:* "Showing the red button yields an Odds Ratio of $e^{0.22} \approx 1.24$. Users exposed to the red button have 24% higher odds of adding an item to their cart."
