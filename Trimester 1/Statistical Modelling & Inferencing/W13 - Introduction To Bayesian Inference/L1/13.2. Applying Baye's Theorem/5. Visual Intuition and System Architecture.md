# 5. Visual Intuition and System Architecture

The transition from a Prior state to a Posterior state through the lens of Likelihood can be viewed as an information processing pipeline.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Initial State] --> B[Prior: P&theta;]
    C[New Sensor/Data Input] --> D[Likelihood: P Data|&theta;]
    
    B --> E{Bayesian Engine}
    D --> E
    
    E -->|Numerator| F[Unnormalized Belief]
    
    G[Law of Total Probability] -->|Calculates P Data| H[Evidence Normalizer]
    
    F --> I[Division Step]
    H --> I
    
    I --> J[Posterior: P &theta;|Data]
    J -.->|Becomes new Prior for next time step| B
```
