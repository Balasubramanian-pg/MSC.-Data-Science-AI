# 3.1.11. The Student's t-Distribution

The t-distribution was developed by William Sealy Gosset while working at Guinness.

He published under the pseudonym "Student" because the company prohibited employees from publishing research.

The t-distribution is:

- symmetric
    
- bell-shaped
    
- centered at zero
    

but compared to the normal distribution:

- heavier tails
    
- more spread
    
- more conservative
    

Why heavier tails?

Because estimating $$\sigma$$ introduces uncertainty.
The t-distribution "admits ignorance" more honestly than the normal distribution.
