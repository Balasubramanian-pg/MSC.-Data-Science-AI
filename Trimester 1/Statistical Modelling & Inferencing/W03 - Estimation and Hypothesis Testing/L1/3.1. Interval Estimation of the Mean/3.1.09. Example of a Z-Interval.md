# 3.1.9. Example of a Z-Interval

Suppose:

- $$\bar{x} = 75 $$ 
- $$\sigma = 12$$
- $$ n = 64$$
- confidence level = 95%

### Step 1: Compute Standard Error

$$
SE = \frac{12}{\sqrt{64}} = \frac{12}{8} = 1.5
$$

### Step 2: Critical Value

$$
Z_{0.025} = 1.96
$$

### Step 3: Margin of Error

$$
E = 1.96(1.5) = 2.94
$$

### Step 4: Confidence Interval

$$
75 \pm 2.94
$$

Final interval:

$$
(72.06,\ 77.94)
$$

Interpretation:

We use a procedure that captures the true mean 95% of the time in repeated sampling.
