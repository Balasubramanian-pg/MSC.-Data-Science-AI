# 3.1.12. Degrees of Freedom

For one-sample mean estimation:

$$
df = n - 1
$$

Degrees of freedom represent how much independent information remains after estimating parameters.

Example:

If $$n = 5$$ and the sample mean is fixed, once four values are chosen, the fifth is forced.

Only four observations vary freely.

Thus:

$$
df = 5 - 1 = 4
$$
