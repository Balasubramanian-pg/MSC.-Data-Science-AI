# 3.1.14. Example of a T-Interval

Suppose:

- $$\bar{x} = 82$$   
- $$s = 10$$
- $$n = 25$$
- confidence level = 95%
    

### Step 1: Degrees of Freedom

$$df = 25 - 1 = 24$$

### Step 2: Critical Value

From t-table:

$$t_{0.025,24} \approx 2.064$$

### Step 3: Standard Error

$$SE = \frac{10}{\sqrt{25}} = 2$$

### Step 4: Margin of Error

$$E = 2.064(2) = 4.128$$

### Step 5: Confidence Interval

$$82 \pm 4.128$$

Final interval:

$$(77.872,\ 86.128)$$
