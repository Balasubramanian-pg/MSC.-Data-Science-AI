# 6.1.6. Comparison with Alternative Edge Detectors

| Method | Noise Handling | Edge Thickness | Connectivity | Computational Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Canny | Excellent (Gaussian + hysteresis) | Thin (NMS) | Strong | Moderate |
| Sobel only | Poor | Thick | Weak | Low |
| Prewitt | Poor | Thick | Weak | Low |
| Laplacian of Gaussian | Good | Thin | Moderate | Moderate |
| Roberts Cross | Poor | Thick | Weak | Very Low |
