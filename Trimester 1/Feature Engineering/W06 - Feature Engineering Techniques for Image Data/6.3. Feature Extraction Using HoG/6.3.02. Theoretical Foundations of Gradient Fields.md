# 6.3.2. Theoretical Foundations of Gradient Fields

The HOG descriptor operates on the principle that the local shape and appearance of an object are characterized by the distribution of intensity gradients.

### 1. Gradient Computation
For each pixel $$(x, y)$$, we compute horizontal and vertical gradients using derivative masks:

$$
g_x(x,y) = I(x+1, y) - I(x-1, y)
$$

$$
g_y(x,y) = I(x, y+1) - I(x, y-1)
$$

These represent the rate of change of intensity across the image grid.

### 2. Magnitude and Orientation
From these components, we derive the gradient magnitude ($$m$$) and orientation ($\theta$):

$$
m(x,y) = \sqrt{g_x(x,y)^2 + g_y(x,y)^2}
$$

$$
\theta(x,y) = \text{atan2}(g_y(x,y), g_x(x,y))
$$

The magnitude $$m(x,y)$$indicates the edge strength, while $$\theta(x,y)$$ indicates the direction of the edge.
