# 6.3.4. Parametric Sensitivity and Mathematical Trade-offs

The performance of a HOG-based model is heavily dependent on three primary hyper-parameters:

| Parameter | Mathematical Role | Effect of Increase |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| `orientations` | Number of angular histogram bins | Higher precision in edge directionality |
| `pixels_per_cell` | Spatial resolution of the descriptor | Smaller values increase detail/noise |
| `cells_per_block` | Normalization region size | Larger regions normalize over more context |

>[!Warning]
> Increasing the `orientations` parameter or decreasing `pixels_per_cell` drastically increases the length of the feature vector. This can lead to the "curse of dimensionality," where the complexity of the machine learning model exceeds the available training data, causing overfitting.
