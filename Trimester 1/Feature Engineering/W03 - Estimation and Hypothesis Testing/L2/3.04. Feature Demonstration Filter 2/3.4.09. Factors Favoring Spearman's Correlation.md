# 3.4.9. Factors Favoring Spearman's Correlation

### 9.1 Non-linear Capability
Rank correlation serves as the optimal filter method when you suspect that independent features influence the target variable in a consistent direction but not at a constant, linear rate. It preserves signals that reside within curved distributions.

### 9.2 Distribution Independence
Unlike purely parametric statistics, rank correlation makes no underlying assumptions about the normal distribution of the data. This non-parametric nature allows it to be deployed immediately on highly skewed real-world datasets without requiring complex mathematical transformations beforehand.
