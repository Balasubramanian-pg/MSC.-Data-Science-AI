# 3.4.2. Theory of Monotonic Relationships

Spearman’s rank correlation evaluates variables based on monotonic relationships rather than linear proportionality. 

A monotonic relationship exists when two variables consistently move in the same relative direction, even if the rate of their change fluctuates. If one variable consistently increases as the other increases, the relationship is monotonically positive. Conversely, if one variable consistently decreases as the other increases, the relationship is monotonically negative.

>[!Note]
> A monotonic function preserves the given order. It never reverses direction, though it may occasionally flatten or accelerate.

For example, the quadratic function for positive values:

$$
y = x^2
$$

This represents a perfect monotonic relationship because as the input increases, the output always increases, even though the trajectory forms a curve rather than a straight line.
