# 3.6.2. The Concept of Entropy

The foundation of information theory relies on quantifying randomness, which is mathematically defined as entropy. 

Entropy serves as a measure of unpredictability within a random variable. If a target variable contains highly balanced, unpredictable classes, it possesses high entropy. If the target variable heavily favors a single, easily predictable outcome, it possesses low entropy. 

In the context of predictive modeling, our fundamental goal is to eliminate this unpredictability. We seek independent features that destroy the entropy of the target variable.
