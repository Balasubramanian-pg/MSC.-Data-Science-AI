# 3.11.4. Feature Selection: Refining the Input Space

Once extraction and construction populate the dataset, the matrix frequently suffers from severe dimensional bloat and multicollinearity. Feature selection is the strict methodological process of pruning this expanded feature set.

By algorithmically retaining only the most informative variables and discarding redundant noise, selection actively reduces computational training costs and aggressively mitigates the risk of mathematical overfitting. The selection process is broadly categorized into three distinct operational methodologies: Filter, Wrapper, and Embedded methods.
