# 3.2.7. Factors Affecting Extraction Efficacy

The success of feature extraction is governed by several underlying forces that dictate the quality of the resulting representations.

### 3.2.7.1 Data Sparsity
As the original dimensionality increases, the volume of the space outpaces the number of observations.
Thus, linear methods like PCA may fail to capture the true manifold of the data, requiring nonlinear techniques like t-SNE or autoencoders.

### 3.2.7.2 Signal-to-Noise Ratio
If the raw data is dominated by random noise, extraction algorithms will inadvertently prioritize this noise as variance.
This leads to extracted features that are highly unstable and prone to overfitting.

### 3.2.7.3 Interpretability Requirements
The choice of extraction method directly impacts the transparency of the final model.
Statistical features remain highly interpretable, whereas deep learned representations sacrifice transparency for predictive power.

The following table summarizes how these factors impact the overall modeling pipeline.

| Factor | Impact on Model Variance | Impact on Interpretability |
| :--- | :---: | ---: |
| Data Sparsity | Very High | Low |
| Signal-to-Noise Ratio | High | Medium |
| Interpretability Requirements | Medium | Very High |

Avoiding these intellectual traps ensures our pursuit of predictive truth remains uncorrupted by structural bias.
