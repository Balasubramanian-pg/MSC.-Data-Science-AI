# 8.0.1. Introduction

Feature engineering has historically been one of the most important and time-consuming stages of machine learning.

Most machine learning algorithms do not learn directly from raw data. Instead, they learn from carefully constructed variables called **features**, which capture patterns, relationships, and predictive signals hidden within the data.

Traditionally, feature engineering has relied heavily on:

* domain expertise
* manual experimentation
* iterative testing
* business knowledge

As datasets become larger and more complex, manual feature creation becomes increasingly difficult to scale.

Automated Feature Engineering (AFE) addresses this challenge by replacing manual feature discovery with algorithmic search and systematic transformation.

> [!Note]
> Automated Feature Engineering represents a shift from human-driven feature creation to algorithm-driven feature discovery.

The goal is not to eliminate human expertise, but to dramatically accelerate the feature engineering process while uncovering patterns that may be difficult for humans to identify manually.
