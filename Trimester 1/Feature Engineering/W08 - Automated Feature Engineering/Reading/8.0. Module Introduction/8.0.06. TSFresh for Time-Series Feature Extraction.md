# 8.0.6. TSFresh for Time-Series Feature Extraction

Time-series datasets contain observations recorded over time.

Examples include:

* stock prices
* sensor readings
* patient monitoring systems
* website traffic
* manufacturing measurements

These datasets require specialized feature extraction techniques.

### 8.0.6.1. What is TSFresh?

TSFresh stands for:

**Time Series Feature Extraction Based on Scalable Hypothesis Tests**

Its objective is to automatically transform raw sequences into informative statistical features.

Instead of analyzing an entire sequence directly, TSFresh summarizes important characteristics of that sequence.

### 8.0.6.2. Types of Extracted Features

TSFresh computes hundreds of statistical descriptors.

Examples include:

#### Distribution Features

$$
\text{Mean}
$$

$$
\text{Variance}
$$

$$
\text{Skewness}
$$

$$
\text{Kurtosis}
$$

#### Frequency Features

Using Fourier analysis:

$$
FFT(x)
$$

Examples include:

* dominant frequencies
* spectral energy
* frequency amplitudes

#### Temporal Features

Examples include:

$$
\text{Autocorrelation}
$$

$$
\text{Partial Autocorrelation}
$$

#### Complexity Features

Examples include:

* entropy
* peak counts
* trend strength
* change points

### 8.0.6.3. Hypothesis Testing for Feature Selection

TSFresh not only generates features but also evaluates their usefulness.

Each generated feature is tested against the target variable.

Conceptually:

$$
H_0:
\text{Feature is unrelated to target}
$$

$$
H_1:
\text{Feature is related to target}
$$

Features demonstrating statistically significant relationships are retained.

Features without predictive signal are discarded.

> [!Tip]
> TSFresh combines automated feature generation with automated statistical validation.
