# 8.5.10. Automated Feature Engineering in Pharmaceutical Analytics

Automated Feature Engineering is increasingly used in healthcare and pharmaceutical analytics.

Examples include:

### 8.5.10.1. Patient Adherence Prediction

Generated features:

- average refill gap
- refill frequency
- therapy duration
- missed prescription count

### 8.5.10.2. HCP Engagement Analytics

Generated features:

- average call frequency
- email open rate
- channel preference score
- engagement trend

### 8.5.10.3. Patient Risk Stratification

Generated features:

- hospitalization frequency
- medication switching rate
- treatment persistence
- disease progression indicators

These derived variables often outperform raw transactional attributes.
