# 8.5.11. Limitations of Automated Feature Engineering

Despite its power, Automated Feature Engineering is not a fully autonomous solution.

Important limitations include:

- computational expense
- feature explosion
- interpretability concerns
- potential overfitting
- dependence on data quality

>[!Warning]
> Automated Feature Engineering cannot compensate for fundamentally poor data quality. Missing values, biased samples, and inaccurate records remain critical problems.
