# 8.5.4. Deep Feature Synthesis (DFS) with Featuretools

One of the most widely used Automated Feature Engineering frameworks is Featuretools.

Featuretools specializes in feature generation from relational datasets.

Examples include:

- customer databases
- sales systems
- healthcare records
- pharmaceutical transactions
- CRM platforms

### 8.5.4.1. The Fundamental Idea

Suppose a business maintains two tables:

**Customers**

| Customer ID | Customer Name |
|----------|----------|
| 101 | Alice |
| 102 | Bob |

**Orders**

| Order ID | Customer ID | Amount |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1 | 101 | 100 |
| 2 | 101 | 200 |
| 3 | 102 | 300 |

Featuretools automatically discovers useful features such as:

$$
\text{SUM(Order Amount)}
$$

$$
\text{MEAN(Order Amount)}
$$

$$
\text{COUNT(Orders)}
$$

Resulting customer-level features become:

| Customer ID | Total Spend | Average Order | Order Count |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| 101 | 300 | 150 | 2 |
| 102 | 300 | 300 | 1 |

### 8.5.4.2. Deep Feature Synthesis (DFS)

Deep Feature Synthesis automatically traverses relationships between tables and applies transformation and aggregation primitives.

The term "deep" refers to stacking multiple operations together.

Example:

First aggregation:

$$
\text{SUM(Order Amount)}
$$

Second transformation:

$$
\log(\text{SUM(Order Amount)})
$$

Third transformation:

$$
\sqrt{\log(\text{SUM(Order Amount)})}
$$

This layered construction creates increasingly sophisticated features.

>[!Note]
> Deep Feature Synthesis can generate hundreds or thousands of candidate features from a relatively small number of source tables.

### 8.5.4.3. Core Components

#### EntitySet

An EntitySet stores:

- tables
- primary keys
- foreign keys
- relationships

It acts as the feature generation environment.

#### Primitives

Primitives are basic operations used for feature construction.

Examples include:

| Primitive Type | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Aggregation | SUM, MEAN, COUNT, MAX |
| Transformation | LOG, ABS, YEAR, MONTH |
| Temporal | DAYS_SINCE, TIME_SINCE |
| Statistical | STD, SKEWNESS |

#### DFS Engine

The DFS engine systematically combines primitives across relationships to generate feature matrices.
