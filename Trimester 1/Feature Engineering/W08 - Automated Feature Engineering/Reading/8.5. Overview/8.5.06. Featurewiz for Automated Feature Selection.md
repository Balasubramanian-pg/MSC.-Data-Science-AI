# 8.5.6. Featurewiz for Automated Feature Selection

Feature generation often produces thousands of variables.

Excessive dimensionality creates challenges such as:

- overfitting
- multicollinearity
- increased computation
- reduced interpretability

Featurewiz focuses on reducing this complexity.

### 8.5.6.1. SULOV Algorithm

SULOV stands for:

**Select Uncorrelated Lowest Variance**

The objective is to eliminate redundant features before model training.

Suppose two variables are highly correlated:

$$
Corr(X_1,X_2)=0.95
$$

Both variables contain similar information.

Keeping both may introduce multicollinearity.

Featurewiz retains the variable with stronger target relevance and removes the weaker one.

### 8.5.6.2. Recursive XGBoost Elimination

After correlation pruning, Featurewiz trains gradient boosting models.

Feature importance scores are computed.

Features with low importance are iteratively removed.

Conceptually:

$$
\text{Feature Importance}
\rightarrow
\text{Rank Features}
\rightarrow
\text{Remove Lowest}
\rightarrow
\text{Retrain}
\rightarrow
\text{Repeat}
$$

This process creates a compact feature set with high predictive value.

### 8.5.6.3. Benefits

Featurewiz provides:

- dimensionality reduction
- improved generalization
- reduced training time
- reduced multicollinearity
- improved model maintainability
