# 8.4.11. Common Misinterpretations

### Interpretation 1

>[!Warning]
> "Automated feature engineering eliminates the need for domain knowledge."

Wrong. Automated tools generate candidates; domain experts must validate and interpret them. A model with uninterpretable features may be accurate but unusable in regulated industries.

### Interpretation 2

>[!Warning]
> "More features always improve model performance."

Wrong. Beyond a certain point, additional features introduce noise, increase training time, and degrade generalization. The goal is parsimony: the smallest feature set that captures the signal.

### Interpretation 3

>[!Warning]
> "Automated selection guarantees generalization."

Wrong. Selection methods optimize on training data. Without proper cross-validation, selected features may reflect spurious correlations that do not replicate in new data.
