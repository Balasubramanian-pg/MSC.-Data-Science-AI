# 8.4.6. FeatureWiz: Model-Driven Selection for Wide Data

FeatureWiz serves as a **gatekeeper for high-dimensional tabular data**.

It combines two selection strategies:

1. **SULOV (Select Uncorrelated Lowest Variance)**: Remove features that are highly correlated with one another but have lower predictive signal

2. **Recursive XGBoost Elimination**: Iteratively train models, remove the weakest features, and retrain

The SULOV method operates on the principle that:

>[!Note]
> Redundant features waste model capacity and increase variance without improving bias.

Mathematically, if two features $$X_i$$ and $$X_j$$ have correlation:

$$
\rho_{ij} = \frac{\text{Cov}(X_i, X_j)}{\sigma_{X_i} \sigma_{X_j}}
$$

and $$\rho_{ij}$$ exceeds a threshold (e.g., $$0.70$$ or $$0.90$$), SULOV retains only the feature with stronger target association and discards the other.
