## 2.5.1. From Raw Distributions to Statistical Symmetry

Real-world numerical data rarely follows an ideal statistical distribution. Variables such as income, transaction amounts, medical measurements, and customer spending often exhibit skewness, heavy tails, and extreme values that can influence statistical analyses and machine learning models.

Attribute transformations are feature engineering techniques that reshape the distribution of numerical variables to improve their statistical properties. By applying mathematical functions such as logarithmic, power, or Yeo-Johnson transformations, these methods reduce skewness, stabilize variance, and minimize the influence of extreme observations.

Transforming feature distributions can improve model performance by producing data that better satisfies the assumptions of many statistical and machine learning algorithms, while also making underlying patterns easier to identify and learn.
