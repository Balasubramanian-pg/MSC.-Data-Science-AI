# 2.6.6. Factors Affecting Proximity Metrics

### 6.1 Scale Discrepancy
Larger numerical ranges lead to massive squared differences in the distance formula.

Thus:
- the dominant feature dictates the nearest neighbors
- smaller scale features are mathematically silenced
- the model learns the geometry of the measurement units rather than the data

### 6.2 Feature Variance
Features with high variance naturally produce larger differences between observations.
This compounds the scale discrepancy, forcing the algorithm to focus exclusively on the most volatile variables.

### 6.3 Algorithmic Sensitivity
Distance-based algorithms are completely blind to the physical meaning of the units.
They rely entirely on the mathematical magnitude of the coordinates provided.
