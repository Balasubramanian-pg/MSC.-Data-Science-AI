# 2.6.2. Why Raw Proximity Metrics Are Fundamentally Flawed

Suppose we are calculating the distance between two patients in a medical dataset.

- Patient A has a cell radius of 15 and a texture value of 0.05.
- Patient B has a cell radius of 20 and a texture value of 0.06.

Neither patient is necessarily an anomaly.

This happens because of scale dominance.

Different scales produce different distance contributions.

The key insight is:

>[!Note]
> A feature with a larger numerical range will disproportionately dominate the distance calculation.

That means the raw distance metric is heavily biased toward the feature with the largest scale.
