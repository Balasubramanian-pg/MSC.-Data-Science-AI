# 2.1.1. From Raw Measurements to Statistical Reality

Look, raw numeric data is an illusion of objectivity. We see a price, a temperature, or a distance, and we assume the machine understands the reality behind it. It does not. 

Algorithms are entirely blind to the physical units of our measurements. They only perceive the geometric distances between numerical coordinates in a high-dimensional space. If you feed them raw, unoptimized numbers, they will confidently learn the geometry of your measurement errors.

Preprocessing is not merely a housekeeping task; it is the act of translating human context into mathematical symmetry. We must strip away the arbitrary scales of our measuring instruments to reveal the true statistical signal underneath.
