# 3. Intuition and Mathematical Formulation

### The TF-IDF Vector Space Model
While N-Grams and count vectors capture term presence, they suffer from the "document length bias" and the "frequent word trap" (words like "the" dominate the vector but carry no semantic signal).

**TF-IDF (Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency)** solves this by scaling the frequency of a term by the information entropy it carries across the entire corpus.

**1. Term Frequency (TF):** Measures local importance. How often does term $t$ appear in document $d$?
$$\text{TF}(t, d) = \frac{f_{t,d}}{\sum_{t' \in d} f_{t',d}}$$

**2. Inverse Document Frequency (IDF):** Measures global rarity. How many documents $N$ contain the term $t$?
$$\text{IDF}(t, D) = \log\left(\frac{N}{1 + |\{d \in D : t \in d\}|}\right) + 1$$
*(Note: The $+1$ smoothing prevents division by zero and zero-valued IDF vectors).*

**3. The Final Vector:**
$$\text{TF-IDF}(t, d, D) = \text{TF}(t, d) \times \text{IDF}(t, D)$$

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Geometric Interpretation:**
> TF-IDF maps every document to a vector in $\mathbb{R}^{|V|}$ where $|V|$ is the vocabulary size. A document discussing "hockey" will have a spike in the dimension corresponding to the word "team" or "hockey", while common dimensions (like "and") are mathematically suppressed towards zero.

### Multinomial Logistic Regression
Once the document is a vector $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{3000}$, we apply a linear classifier. For multi-class problems (like the 20 Newsgroups dataset), we use the Softmax function to output a probability distribution over the $K$ classes.

$$P(y=k | \mathbf{x}) = \frac{\exp(\mathbf{w}_k^T \mathbf{x} + b_k)}{\sum_{j=1}^K \exp(\mathbf{w}_j^T \mathbf{x} + b_j)}$$

Where:
*   $\mathbf{w}_k$ is the weight vector for class $k$.
*   $b_k$ is the bias for class $k$.

The model learns that high values in the "puck" and "ice" dimensions of $\mathbf{x}$ should strongly activate the weight vector $\mathbf{w}_{\text{hockey}}$.
