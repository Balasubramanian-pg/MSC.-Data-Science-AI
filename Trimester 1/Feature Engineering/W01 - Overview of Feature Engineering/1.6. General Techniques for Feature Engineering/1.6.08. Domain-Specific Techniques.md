# 1.6.8. Domain-Specific Techniques

Different data types require specialized engineering approaches.

Text data utilizes tokenization, Bag of Words, TF-IDF, and word embeddings to convert unstructured text into numeric vectors.

Image data employs pixel normalization, edge detection, and features derived from pre-trained model layers.

Time series data focuses on temporal structures such as lag features, rolling averages, and time-based splits.

Understanding the domain context dictates which specific technique will yield the highest predictive signal.
