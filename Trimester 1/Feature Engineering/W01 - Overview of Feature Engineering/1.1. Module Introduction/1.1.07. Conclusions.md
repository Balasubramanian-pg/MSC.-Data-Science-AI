# 1.1.7. Conclusions

We must abandon the seductive notion that algorithms are magic. They are merely mirrors, reflecting the quality of the data we present to them. 

Feature engineering is the discipline of polishing that mirror. It requires us to think deeply about the nature of our data, to challenge our assumptions about what constitutes a variable, and to relentlessly pursue the simplest, most expressive representation of reality.

When you construct a feature, ask yourself: does this expose a true underlying pattern, or am I merely adding noise to the void? 

>[!Tip]
> "The master of machine learning is not the one who knows the most algorithms, but the one who knows how to ask the best questions of the data."
