# 1.1.5. Step-by-Step Feature Construction Example

Suppose:
- We possess a raw dataset of customer records.
- We have a `DOB` (Date of Birth) column formatted as a string.
- Our objective is to predict the likelihood of a customer purchasing a specific product.

### Step 1: Parse the Temporal String
Convert the raw string representation of the date into a recognized datetime object to unlock its mathematical properties.

### Step 2: Extract the Relevant Component
Isolate the year of birth from the datetime object, discarding the day and month which may introduce unnecessary noise for this specific macro-level prediction.

### Step 3: Define the Transformation Logic
Establish the mathematical relationship between the current temporal state and the extracted birth year:

$$
\text{Age} = \text{Current Year} - \text{Birth Year}
$$

### Step 4: Apply the Transformation
Execute the calculation across the entire column, generating a new, continuous numerical feature.

### Step 5: Validate the Output
Inspect the new `Age` column to ensure no null values were introduced and that the distribution aligns with logical human demographics, thereby confirming the feature is ready for model ingestion.
