# 1.3.1. The Nature of the Iris Benchmark

The Iris dataset stands as a foundational benchmark in the realm of machine learning, comprising 150 samples of iris flowers. It is a balanced tapestry of nature, offering 50 samples for each of the three species: *Setosa*, *Versicolor*, and *Virginica*. Each specimen is characterized by four continuous morphological features: sepal length, sepal width, petal length, and petal width.

We must ask ourselves: why do we begin with such a pristine, balanced dataset devoid of missing values? It is because before we can conquer the chaos of the real world, we must first understand the pure mechanics of classification in a controlled environment. This dataset serves as the crucible in which our feature engineering theories are first tested.
