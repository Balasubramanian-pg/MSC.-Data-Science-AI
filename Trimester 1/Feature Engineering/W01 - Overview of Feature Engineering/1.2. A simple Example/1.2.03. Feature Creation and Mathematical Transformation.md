# 1.2.3. Feature Creation and Mathematical Transformation

This is the core feature engineering step. 

The goal is to reduce the dimensionality of the data from two features down to one highly predictive feature by combining them mathematically. We derive a new variable, the Average, by taking the arithmetic mean of the two existing continuous variables.

The transformation formula is defined as:

$$
X_{\text{new}} = \frac{X_1 + X_2}{2}
$$

where:

- $$X_{\text{new}}$$ = the newly engineered Average feature

- $$X_1$$ = the Math mark

- $$X_2$$ = the Science mark

By averaging the scores, we assume that overall academic performance is a stronger signal for the target label than either subject evaluated independently. Let us reiterate the core transformation formula, as it is the foundation of this dimensionality reduction:

$$
X_{\text{new}} = \frac{X_1 + X_2}{2}
$$

With the new feature constructed, we must evaluate its ability to separate the target classes.
