# 4.1.6. Explained Variance Ratio

To determine the optimal number of components to retain, we analyze the cumulative explained variance.

The variance explained by any single component is calculated as:

$$
\text{Explained Variance} = \frac{\lambda_j}{\sum_{i=1}^{p} \lambda_i}
$$

where:

- $$\lambda_j$$= eigenvalue of the$$j$$-th component
- $$\sum_{i=1}^{p} \lambda_i$$ = sum of all computed eigenvalues

We typically select the top $$k$$ components such that the cumulative explained variance reaches a high threshold, often between 0.90 and 0.95.
