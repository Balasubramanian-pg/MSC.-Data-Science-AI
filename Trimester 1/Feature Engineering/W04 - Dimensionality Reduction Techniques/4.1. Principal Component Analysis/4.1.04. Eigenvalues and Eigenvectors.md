# 4.1.4. Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

To find the optimal directions of maximum variance, we perform spectral decomposition on the sample covariance matrix. 

This decomposition satisfies the following equation:

$$
S v = \lambda v
$$

where:

- $$S$$ = covariance matrix
- $$v$$ = eigenvector 
- $$\lambda$$ = eigenvalue 

The eigenvectors define the new spatial axes (the Principal Components) in the feature space. The corresponding eigenvalues represent the absolute magnitude of variance captured by each specific eigenvector.
