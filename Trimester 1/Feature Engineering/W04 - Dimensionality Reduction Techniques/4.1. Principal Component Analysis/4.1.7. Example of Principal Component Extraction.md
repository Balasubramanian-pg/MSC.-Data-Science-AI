# 4.1.7. Example of Principal Component Extraction

Suppose:

- $$X_1$$and$$X_2$$ are already standardized continuous features
- Variance of $$X_1$$ is 1.0
- Variance of $$X_2$$ is 1.0
- Covariance between the features is 0.8
- We wish to extract the first Principal Component

### Step 1: Compute Covariance Matrix
$$
S = \begin{bmatrix} 1.0 & 0.8 \\ 0.8 & 1.0 \end{bmatrix}
$$

### Step 2: Calculate Eigenvalues
$$
(1.0 - \lambda)^2 - 0.64 = 0
$$
yielding $$\lambda_1 = 1.8$$and$$\lambda_2 = 0.2$$

### Step 3: Calculate Principal Eigenvector
$$
v_1 = [0.707, 0.707]
$$

### Step 4: Compute Explained Variance
$$
\text{Explained Variance} = \frac{1.8}{1.8 + 0.2} = 0.90
$$

### Step 5: Formulate the First Principal Component
**$$PC_1 = 0.707 X_1 + 0.707 X_2$$**
