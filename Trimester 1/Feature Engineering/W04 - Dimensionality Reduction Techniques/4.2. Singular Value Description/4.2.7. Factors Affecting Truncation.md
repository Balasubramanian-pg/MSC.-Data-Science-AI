# 4.2.7. Factors Affecting Truncation

The choice of the hyperparameter $$k$$ dictates the fundamental trade-off between information retention and dimensionality reduction.

### 7.1 Higher Component Count
Setting a larger $$k$$ retains a greater percentage of the original mathematical detail. However, it preserves higher dimensionality and risks retaining low-importance singular values that correspond strictly to noise.

### 7.2 Lower Component Count
Setting a smaller $$k$$ aggressively reduces dimensionality. This accelerates model training times and enforces strict noise filtering, but it risks discarding critical latent nuances required for accurate statistical prediction.
