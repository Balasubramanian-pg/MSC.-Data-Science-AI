# 7.1.2. Why Static Feature Engineering Is Fundamentally Incomplete

Suppose two analysts model the same stock price series.

- Analyst A treats each day's closing price as an independent observation and fits a standard regression

- Analyst B incorporates yesterday's price as a predictor

Analyst B's model will almost certainly outperform Analyst A's.

This happens because of **autocorrelation**: correlation between a variable and its own lagged values. The key insight is:

>[!Note]
> A time series observation is not a random draw from a population. It is a realization of a stochastic process evolving through time.

That means the sample at time $$t$$ has its own probability structure conditional on history. The past directly influences the present.
