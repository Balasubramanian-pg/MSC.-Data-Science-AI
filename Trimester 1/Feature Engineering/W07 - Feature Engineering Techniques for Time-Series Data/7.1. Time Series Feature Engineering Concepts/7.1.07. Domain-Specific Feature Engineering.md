# 7.1.7. Domain-Specific Feature Engineering

### 7.1 Finance: Market Indicators

Financial time series demand specialized features that encode market dynamics.

#### 7.1.1 Moving Averages

The simple moving average (SMA) over $$w$$ periods smooths price paths:

$$
\text{SMA}_t^{(w)} = \frac{1}{w} \sum_{i=0}^{w-1} P_{t-i}
$$

where $$P_t$$= price at time$$t$$. Common windows include 14-day, 50-day, and 200-day averages.

#### 7.1.2 Relative Strength Index (RSI)

RSI is a momentum oscillator measuring speed and magnitude of price movements:

$$
\text{RSI} = 100 - \frac{100}{1 + \text{RS}}
$$

where:

$$
\text{RS} = \frac{\text{Average Gain over } w \text{ periods}}{\text{Average Loss over } w \text{ periods}}
$$

Interpretation thresholds:

| RSI Value | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| RSI > 70 | Overbought condition |
| RSI < 30 | Oversold condition |
| 30 ≤ RSI ≤ 70 | Neutral territory |

#### 7.1.3 Bollinger Bands

Bollinger Bands visualize expected price variation using mean $$\pm$$ 2 standard deviations:

$$
\text{Upper Band}_t = \text{SMA}_t^{(w)} + 2\sigma_t^{(w)}
$$

$$
\text{Lower Band}_t = \text{SMA}_t^{(w)} - 2\sigma_t^{(w)}
$$

where:

- $$\text{SMA}_t^{(w)}$$= moving average at time$$t$$

- $$\sigma_t^{(w)}$$ = rolling standard deviation at time $$t$$

Prices touching or exceeding the bands signal unusual volatility or potential reversal points.

### 7.2 Healthcare: Physiological Signals

Physiological time series require features that capture cardiac and autonomic function.

#### 7.2.1 RR Intervals

The RR interval measures time between consecutive cardiac R-peaks:

$$
\text{RR}_i = t_{R_{i+1}} - t_{R_i}
$$

where:

- $$t_{R_i}$$ = timestamp of the $$i$$-th R-peak

- $$\text{RR}_i$$ = interval between peak $$i$$ and peak $$i+1$$

Heart rate in beats per minute is derived as:

$$
\text{HR}_i = \frac{60,000}{\text{RR}_i \text{ (in milliseconds)}}
$$

#### 7.2.2 Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

HRV quantifies variation in RR intervals, reflecting autonomic nervous system health. Greater HRV typically indicates healthier cardiac regulation.

#### 7.2.3 RMSSD (Root Mean Square of Successive Differences)

RMSSD captures rapid beat-to-beat variations:

$$
\text{RMSSD} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} (\text{RR}_{i+1} - \text{RR}_i)^2}
$$

where:

- $$n$$ = number of RR intervals

- $$\text{RR}_{i+1} - \text{RR}_i$$ = successive difference

RMSSD is particularly sensitive to parasympathetic (vagal) activity and short-term physiological changes.

#### 7.2.4 Pre/Post-RR Features

Contextual features surrounding an event (e.g., an arrhythmia) provide temporal grounding:

$$
\text{Pre-RR} = \text{RR interval immediately before the event}
$$

$$
\text{Post-RR} = \text{RR interval immediately after the event}
$$

The ratio or difference between pre and post intervals helps classify rhythm abnormalities.
